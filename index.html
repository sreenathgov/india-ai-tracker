Kanan Labs · Company Roadmap

We began by modelling
the trade world. Now we are putting
that intelligence to work.

Kanan Labs is being built in deliberate stages: first the knowledge infrastructure, then its deployment inside live trade workflows, and ultimately a continuous intelligence layer across global value chains.

TradeWatch is the first commercial expression of that infrastructure. SectorWatch is the horizon.

  1. 01 March 2026 · Foundation

    DRONA begins modelling the trade world.

    Kanan Labs is founded around a structured, source-linked model of products, rules, jurisdictions, documents, parties and evidence. Accretive World Learning expands that model with every verified cycle. A source-linked world model that expands through Accretive World Learning.

  2. 02 Design partners and pilots

    TradeWatch enters live workflows.

    Inside defined products, corridors and shipment workflows, TradeWatch operates first in shadow mode—testing what applies and preparing evidence-backed actions for human review. Pilots test live shipments in shadow mode before human review. Discuss a pilot

  3. 03 Expansion · Connected execution

    Compliance becomes collaborative.

    With customer authorisation, TradeWatch coordinates evidence, decisions and approvals across suppliers, brokers, logistics providers, insurers and banks—preserving every exchange in one audit trail. TradeWatch coordinates evidence and approvals across trade partners in one audit trail.

  4. 04 Horizon · SectorWatch

    From shipment readiness to continuous risk intelligence.

    The same infrastructure connects regulatory change, supplier exposure and geopolitical pressure across the value chain—turning the accumulated trade world model into continuous intelligence. SectorWatch turns the trade world model into continuous regulatory and geopolitical intelligence.

Trade has become fragmented and evidence‑intensive. The next era will be defined by a company’s ability to maintain a defensible, continuously updated evidence position across customs, insurance, finance, regulation, and supply‑chain risk.
Kanan is building the intelligence layer for that problem.

Sreenath Govindarajan

Founder, Kanan Labs

Cross-border law · AI governance · Regulatory strategy · Trade intelligence

Businesses experience regulation in fragments. Most AI systems reason about it in fragments too.

I founded Kanan Labs after seeing the same structural failure from two directions.

Frontier AI could retrieve extraordinary amounts of information, yet struggled to connect products, supply chains, jurisdictions, institutions, rules and evidence into one coherent model. Industry faced the corresponding problem: businesses could not see the regulations, incentives and emerging risks relevant to them as one usable operating picture.

Kanan Labs was founded to close both gaps.

DRONA builds a structured model of the trade world through Accretive World Learning. TradeWatch is its first commercial expression—turning that intelligence into action inside live cross-border workflows.

  1. I

    Model before answering

    The system must understand how the world fits together before it reasons about any part of it.

  2. II

    Evidence before action

    Every material conclusion preserves its source, rule, reasoning path and human review.

  3. III

    A narrow entry, a larger horizon

    TradeWatch begins with shipment-level compliance. The larger undertaking is continuous intelligence for regulated global value chains.
