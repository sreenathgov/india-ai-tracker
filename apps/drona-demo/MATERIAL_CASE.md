## Inquiry and branches

1. Is the entire required billet lot usable and allocated at Deccan? Yes: scoped on-site cover; No: investigate dealer stock.
2. Has the dealer reserved unaffected approved local stock with usable receipt by 12 Nov? Yes: sample reservation and 11 Nov receipt plan; No: check latest shipment timing.
3. Does the updated shipment plan still put usable receipt after 12 Nov? Yes: timing-gap forecast supported; No: sample revised 10–11 Nov usable-receipt plan challenges the original forecast.

Unknown, declined and awaiting stay separate from No and remain on their current question. Partial or unconfirmed cover is not treated as full protection. Call preparation explains the owner, requested evidence and meaning of each answer. Recording a sample reply adds an authored source and observation, updates the assessment version and reprojects every dependent surface. It does not make a call, attach a real document, book capacity, pay a supplier or execute an action.

Protected paths retain future execution uncertainty. The late path provides a proposed recovery brief: confirm grade/quantity/availability, preserve approved cover before 6 Nov, or assess partial supply and resequencing with planning. Finance is not treated as a cure for a physical delay without evidence of a payment-dependent feasible response.
