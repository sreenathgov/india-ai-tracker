## Objective and audience

Make the viewer's understanding deliberate. At every moment the visitor should know **where to look, what they are seeing, why it matters, and what to do next**.

The immediate audience is investors, accelerators and competition reviewers; prospective customers are also an audience. They should understand Drona's order-specific decision process without a presenter. The experience is a directed demonstration with bounded meaningful interactions. The earlier 75–90 second video discussion is not a mandatory timer for the interactive walkthrough.

Polish both the underlying product screen and its explanation. A brighter spotlight cannot compensate for a confusing screen. Each component included in the founder's storyboard must earn its place by answering the viewer's question at that moment.

- **Act One:** explain the dashboard step by step, beginning with the map.
- **Act Two:** enter the featured case file and explain the investigation.
- Sreenath owns the detailed storyboard and order of the remaining beats. Do not impose a final step count or add a tour of every available feature.

Across that sequence, make this reasoning intelligible:

**Production commitment → relevant supplier or component dependency → possible consequence → evidence needed → targeted question → updated assessment and useful next action.**

## Specification for every beat

Before implementation, identify:

| Element | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Takeaway | What one thing should the viewer understand? |
| Target | Which actual component supplies that understanding? |
| Explanation | What does it show, and why does it matter to the order? |
| Interaction | Is this an observation followed by Continue, or a meaningful action on the real product control? |
| Result | What visibly changes, and what should the viewer infer from that change? |
| Recovery | How do Back, Exit, replay, unavailable targets and alternate answers behave? |
| Access | How will keyboard, screen-reader and compact-screen users receive the same explanation and action? |

## Attention, layout and motion

- One focal area at a time. Dim unrelated content while retaining recognisable page geography.
- Put the explanation beside the target on wide screens and in a reserved region on compact screens. Keep the target, explanation and navigation unobstructed.
- Show the essential purpose immediately; do not bury it under “Why this step?”. Reserve optional details for supporting evidence or secondary explanations.
- Distinguish headline, supporting explanation, status and action through typography, spacing and grouping. Avoid tiny essential text, excessive labels and equally weighted blocks.
- Use restrained, consistent colours. Keep textual status labels; colour alone must not carry risk or evidence state.
- Keep the real target mounted. Do not clone or simulate it to create a more convenient tour.
- Let the viewer control pace. Advance interactive stages after the expected application state completes, not from a timer or arbitrary click count. Explain a resulting change before moving on.
- Provide coherent Back, Exit and Replay. Revisiting an explanation must not silently rewrite recorded evidence.
- Reveal the mask and explanation together once positioned. Avoid repeated pulsing, unnecessary camera movement and detached or jumping callouts. Respect reduced motion.

## Language and analytical integrity

Use calm, precise, restrained and invitational institutional language. Lead with the implication or purpose, use concrete nouns and active verbs, and explain the operational reason for the next action. Institutional language should remain easy to understand. Avoid hype, inflated AI claims, ornamental jargon and unsupported certainty.

The founder explicitly selected **“digital twin”** for the map introduction. Do not revert it to “digital model” or repeatedly reopen that wording decision.

Preserve Drona's order-specific analytical unit and the fictional demonstration boundary. In the featured regulatory case:

- A public-rule match raises an inquiry; shipment scope and importer readiness remain separate checks.
- Missing evidence does not establish a confirmed gap. Unknown, refused and delayed remain distinct from No.
- Dependent units are not automatically delayed units. A linked order value is not an estimated financial loss.
- Evidence may confirm, weaken or close the concern; do not force every branch to the adverse outcome.
- Display monetary value and useful response time according to their actual evidence status; retain “Awaiting master data” and “Unconfirmed” where appropriate.
- Show relevant multi-tier relationships when the case establishes them. Do not infer causal exposure merely from a map line or location.
- Keep actions integrated into Overview. Do not restore the removed Actions tab.
- “Ask Drona” uses authored supplier-specific interactions in this demo. Live AI, audio, supplier calls and executed mitigation are not connected capabilities.
- Keep illustrative companies, authored order data and sample replies clear. Do not imply quality-defect prediction, a legal determination, measured predictive performance or a live customer deployment.

The first implementation used 62% dimming and a conspicuous white map frame. Sreenath rejected that visual treatment. The refined version received “Much better” and is the current local baseline:

- 48% neutral-charcoal veil: `rgba(17, 19, 18, .48)`.
- Clean cutout at the map's actual bounds and computed corner radius. No bright outline, inner glow or exposed pale padding ring. Earlier 6–8px clear-padding advice was not retained because it would expose the light dashboard and recreate the white rim.
- Explanation card maximum width 360px, cream surface, 28px Georgia heading and 15px supporting text.
- 16px separation beside the map on desktop; 20px beneath it on compact layouts.
- Soft card shadow: `0 8px 28px rgba(12, 16, 16, .13)`.
- Coordinated 200ms entrance after positioning; reduced motion removes the fade.
- Initial focus on the reading heading. Controls retain visible keyboard focus when reached.
- Continue, Back and Exit remain reachable. On short screens the explanatory reading region scrolls.

44%, 48% and 52% candidates were captured and compared; 48% is the running default. These values are Drona design choices, not universal professional standards. Compact map framing remains the existing implementation and has not been redesigned by this increment.
