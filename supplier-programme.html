01 · The risk

A real order can still stop before production starts.

Late working capital can delay materials, production and delivery. War can raise oil and freight costs. A new tariff can affect a material or shipment.

02 · How Kanan helps

We help you show the bank how you will deliver.

We organise the facts behind your order, materials and production plan—and help answer bank questions.

Selected suppliers receive hands-on support at no charge.

03 · What happens next

Three steps. One clear next move.

  1. 01Apply

    Tell us what you make and how much working capital you need.

  2. 02Speak

    We call within 24 hours to understand the requirement.

  3. 03Prepare

    If selected, we prepare the case and coordinate with your chosen bank—with your permission.

No bank, tax or identity documents are requested now.

04 · Who should apply

Do not let finance limit the order you can take.

If you manufacture in India and need working capital, apply. Foundries, casting, fabrication, forging, machining and other manufacturers are welcome.

A purchase order helps, but you can still apply without one.

Before you apply

Five quick answers.

01Is Kanan’s support free?

Yes, for selected suppliers. The loan itself is not free; your bank sets all terms.

02Does Kanan approve the loan?

No. Your bank or NBFC makes the final decision.

03What documents do I need now?

None. Do not send bank, tax or identity documents with this form.

04Can I apply without a purchase order?

Yes. Tell us whether you have an order, customer schedule or expected order.

05What happens to my information?

We use it to review and respond to your application. Retention details are available in the Privacy Policy.
