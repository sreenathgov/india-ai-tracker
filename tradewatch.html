Frequently asked

Questions about TradeWatch.

01 What is TradeWatch?

TradeWatch is Kanan Labs’ agentic trade-compliance service for manufacturers and exporters. It helps teams determine what applies to a shipment, prepare the required evidence and documentation, and coordinate outstanding inputs with approved suppliers, CHAs, and brokers.

Material findings remain linked to their supporting evidence, regulatory basis, and customer approval, leaving a clear audit trail.

02 What trade-compliance capabilities does TradeWatch provide?

TradeWatch supports HS classification; tariff and duty assessment; FTA and scheme eligibility; country-of-origin determination and Certificate of Origin evidence; customs and marine-insurance evidence packets; and supplier and broker collaboration.

Supporting capabilities include automatic document classification, source-linked field extraction, cross-document reconciliation, and regulatory screening. Exact coverage is confirmed for each product, shipment lane, and engagement scope.

03 Is TradeWatch suitable for both growing exporters and large manufacturers?

Yes. Growing exporters can use selected TradeWatch capabilities to reduce compliance research, documentation, reconciliation, and follow-up without deploying a large enterprise trade system.

For large manufacturers, TradeWatch can support multiple product families and shipment lanes while coordinating approved evidence across internal teams, suppliers, and trade partners. The deployment is configured around the company’s supply chain, systems, and level of operational complexity.

04 Which jurisdictions and trade lanes does TradeWatch support?

TradeWatch is designed for cross-border trade across jurisdictions. India, the European Union, and the United States are among the initial jurisdictions being developed, with APAC and other markets added according to customer requirements.

Kanan can onboard a customer regardless of where it sources from or ships to. During onboarding, we map the customer’s product families, origin and destination countries, applicable authorities, and shipment lanes, then configure the relevant compliance scope.

05 How does TradeWatch work with existing systems and email?

TradeWatch operates alongside the systems and partners a company already uses. Depending on the agreed setup, it can work with designated email threads, uploaded documents, Excel, Tally, Zoho, SAP, and approved integrations.

TradeWatch does not read an entire inbox to discover shipments automatically. A user assigns or flags the shipment or compliance task, controls the information made available, and approves material outputs before external release.

06 How can TradeWatch reduce compliance costs and manual effort?

TradeWatch targets the time spent locating documents, re-entering information, checking inconsistencies, following up with suppliers and brokers, correcting incomplete packets, and identifying missed FTA or scheme opportunities.

The exact savings depend on shipment volume, product complexity, trade lanes, and the company’s existing process. During a pilot, Kanan establishes the current baseline and measures changes in preparation time, discrepancies, rework, and turnaround. Quantified savings are presented only after they have been validated with the customer.

07 How is TradeWatch different from conventional trade-compliance or document-automation software?

Many tools concentrate on one layer of the problem, such as regulatory content, document extraction, record-keeping, or customs filing. TradeWatch is designed around completing an assigned compliance task: determining requirements, preparing evidence, coordinating missing inputs, and recording the approval trail behind the result.

It can operate alongside existing ERP, global trade management, and document systems. The practical difference is agentic compliance execution with traceability—not another dashboard for the customer to manage.

08 How does TradeWatch make its outputs auditable and defensible?

Each material determination is recorded with the supporting document and precise source reference, the applicable requirement, any unresolved ambiguity, and the relevant customer review or approval.

Where the rules and available evidence permit a clear determination, TradeWatch uses controlled and repeatable reasoning. Where information is incomplete or professional judgement is required, the matter is flagged for human review. This allows the customer to understand what was concluded, why it was concluded, and what evidence supports it.

09 Does TradeWatch replace CHAs, freight forwarders, insurance brokers, or internal compliance teams?

No. TradeWatch is designed to improve the work performed with these parties, not remove them.

It prepares and reconciles the information required for review, coordinates approved follow-ups, and produces evidence packages for the relevant team or trade partner. The customer remains the human approval gate, while authorised professionals retain responsibility for regulated advice, filing, placement, and other activities within their respective roles.

10 Who can participate in a TradeWatch pilot?

TradeWatch is entering a limited number of controlled pilot and design-partner engagements. Manufacturers and exporters are the primary users. Kanan is also seeking participation from CHAs, freight forwarders, logistics providers, and marine-insurance brokers as workflow and integration partners.

A pilot normally begins with a selected product family, trade lane, and representative set of shipments operated in shadow mode. The parties agree on the required outputs and success measures before evaluating the results and deciding whether to expand the engagement.
