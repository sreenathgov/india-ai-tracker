About the role

A rule engine is only as good as the reading of the rules underneath it. Someone has to know what a rule of origin actually requires, where CBIC's position diverges from the tariff text, and which of two plausible classifications a customs officer will accept.

This role is that person. You will sit between the primary sources and the engineering team, translating legal instruments into structured logic and holding the line when a shortcut would be technically convenient and substantively wrong. You will also carry a share of Kanan Labs' published analysis.

What you’ll do

  • Read and interpret primary instruments — tariff schedules, notifications, FTAs, rules of origin, licensing conditions.
  • Translate them into structured rule specifications the engineering team can encode without ambiguity.
  • Review system outputs against your own reading and escalate divergences.
  • Build and maintain the known-answer case library that the evaluation harness runs against.
  • Write for Kanan Labs publications — analysis of policy changes and their operational consequences.
  • Sit in on customer conversations and bring back what the rules mean in practice.

What we look for

  • Three or more years in customs, trade compliance, indirect tax, trade law, or a customs brokerage.
  • Working knowledge of HS classification, rules of origin, and Indian customs procedure.
  • The ability to read a legal instrument closely and say precisely what it does and does not cover.
  • Clear written English — you will be publishing under the company's name.
  • Comfort working with engineers and being asked to make your reasoning explicit.

Nice to have

  • Experience with FTA origin determination or advance-ruling practice.
  • Exposure to trade compliance software, however unsatisfying.
  • A second jurisdiction beyond India.
