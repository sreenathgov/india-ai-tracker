About the role

Kanan Labs has a visual language already: navy and cream, Cormorant against Inter, institutional restraint, no stock photography, no gradients standing in for ideas. It works, and it is currently held together by one person's judgement rather than a system.

This role takes it over and makes it a system — a design language with rules, applied consistently across TradeWatch, the India AI Tracker, SectorWatch and the publications. You will design the product surfaces where the hardest information density lives, and you will have real authority over what ships.

What you’ll do

  • Own the design system: type, colour, spacing, motion, components, and the rules governing them.
  • Design TradeWatch's core surfaces alongside the product engineer — evidence, review, timeline.
  • Design the published research surface: article layouts, data presentation, dossier covers.
  • Set the motion language and its accessibility floor, including reduced-motion behaviour.
  • Hold the visual line across every surface the company ships.

What we look for

  • A portfolio with product work in it — dense, functional interfaces, not just marketing pages.
  • Command of typography. This is not negotiable for this role.
  • Experience building and documenting a design system others actually followed.
  • Comfort working in code, or at least reading it well enough to specify precisely.
  • A point of view about restraint that you can defend.

Nice to have

  • Editorial or publication design background.
  • Data visualisation work.
  • Motion design with an accessibility discipline behind it.
