About the role

TradeWatch is where the reasoning becomes usable. A compliance manager opens it under time pressure, needs to understand why a classification was proposed, needs to disagree with it, and needs to leave with something they can put in front of a customs officer.

That is a product problem with an unusually high bar on clarity. This role owns it end to end — interface, application logic, and the API contract behind it — working closely with the designer and the knowledge-systems engineer.

What you’ll do

  • Build the TradeWatch interface: classification review, evidence packets, shipment timelines, broker handoff.
  • Own the API contract between the product surface and the reasoning core.
  • Make uncertainty legible — a confident answer and a hedged one must not look the same.
  • Instrument the product so we learn what compliance teams actually do with it.
  • Hold the line on accessibility and performance; both are requirements, not polish.

What we look for

  • Three or more years building product interfaces for a real workflow, not a marketing site.
  • Strong TypeScript and modern frontend; comfort in the backend when the contract needs changing.
  • Demonstrated care about how information is presented under time pressure.
  • Experience with accessibility as a build-time discipline.
  • Taste. Show us something you built that you are still proud of.

Nice to have

  • Background in a regulated or high-consequence domain — finance, healthcare, legal, logistics.
  • Experience designing for expert users rather than consumers.
  • Comfort with data visualisation that is not decorative.
