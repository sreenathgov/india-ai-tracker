About the role

Every claim DRONA makes traces back to a primary source: a CBIC notification, a DGFT circular, an HS schedule revision, a partner-country tariff table. Those sources arrive as PDFs, malformed HTML, scanned annexures and undocumented APIs, and they change without warning.

This role builds the pipelines that make them dependable. Not a scraping job — an ingestion, normalisation and provenance job, where the hardest requirement is that we can always say where a number came from and which version of the truth it belonged to.

What you’ll do

  • Build and operate ingestion pipelines for tariff schedules, trade notifications, and customs data across jurisdictions.
  • Normalise heterogeneous sources into the canonical schema, preserving citation and effective-date provenance throughout.
  • Build change detection that catches a silently amended notification before a customer does.
  • Own data quality: validation gates, reconciliation checks, and alerting that someone actually reads.
  • Keep the cost and latency of the pipeline honest as source count grows.

What we look for

  • Three or more years on production data pipelines, including sources you did not control and could not fix.
  • Strong Python and SQL; comfort with document parsing and the reality of bad PDFs.
  • A habit of building validation before building throughput.
  • Experience with schema evolution and versioned datasets.
  • Care about provenance as a first-class field, not an afterthought.

Nice to have

  • Exposure to customs, logistics, or government data sources in India or elsewhere.
  • Experience with OCR pipelines or table extraction from scanned documents.
  • Familiarity with orchestration tooling and its failure modes.
