How we hire

A short process, and a reply either way.

  1. 01

    You apply

    The form sits at the foot of each role: three written questions, your links, and a CV if you have one available. It should take about half an hour.

  2. 02

    We review it

    Every application is read by a person, and you will have a reply within a week either way. The written answers carry more weight than the CV.

  3. 03

    Two conversations

    One covering work you have already done, one covering the work here. Both are technical, and neither is a puzzle interview. Where the role calls for it, a paid exercise follows — real work, and you keep it.

If none of these roles fit.

We would still be glad to hear from you, particularly if you have worked inside customs, freight or trade policy and have a considered view of where the existing software falls short. Tell us what you would want to build here.
