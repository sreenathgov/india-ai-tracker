Supplier Programme and finance

Kanan is not a bank, non-banking financial company or lender. It does not make underwriting decisions, promise approval, determine pricing or security, rank institutions for an applicant, handle loan funds, or receive a percentage of financing through this programme. A financial institution independently determines eligibility, terms, conditions and approval.

Submitting an application creates no borrower–lender, agency, brokerage, advisory or fiduciary relationship. Kanan may help a selected supplier organize evidence and, only after separate authorization, coordinate questions with the supplier’s chosen institution. A stronger or clearer evidence package may expose adverse facts, produce no decision change, or lead to tighter terms.

External-risk diagnostic

A Kanan external-risk diagnostic is informational, non-exhaustive and based on sources, assumptions and facts available at a point in time. It is not a prediction, certification, credit rating, guarantee of production or assurance of delivery. External events may develop differently, and supplier-specific effects depend on actual inputs, sources, contracts, routes, controls and mitigations.

TradeWatch, trackers and research

Kanan’s intelligence products support discovery and decision preparation. They do not replace a licensed customs broker, lawyer, insurer, accountant or another qualified professional. The India AI Tracker aggregates publicly available developments and is not affiliated with a government body, regulator, publisher or commercial entity unless expressly stated.

Accuracy, sources and intellectual property

We aim to represent sources accurately but cannot guarantee completeness, currency or fitness for a particular decision. Third-party content remains the property of its publisher. Links and limited summaries are provided for research and discovery. Views in a third-party source do not become Kanan’s views by inclusion.

Corrections and removal requests

To report an error or request review of source inclusion, write to feedback@kananlabs.in with the relevant page and evidence. Reasonable requests will be reviewed promptly.
